# Example models

Shapes for trying Lamina. The synthetic ones are generated by the project and carry no terms of their own. The
scanned ones come from research repositories and are redistributed here under their terms, which allow free
redistribution and non-commercial use with acknowledgement: cut them for yourself, not for sale. For anything you
intend to sell, use your own model or the synthetic ones.

| files | source | terms |
|---|---|---|
| `blob`, `bonsai`, `bowl`, `bracket`, `cone`, `cube`, `cylinder`, `droplet`, `dumbbell`, `egg`, `figure`, `pear`, `pyramid`, `snowman`, `sphere`, `tall_bar`, `torus`, `tube`, `twisted`, `wavy_torus`, `shade` (a lamp shade: stacked acrylic layers on 3D-printed standoffs), `wedge`, `paper_lamp` (the five-leg lantern: twelve dodecahedra as a card lamp, folded panels, one window per pentagon closed with paper, the hub's bottom open for the bulb's cable), `fold_test`, `fold_box` and `fold_lamp` (folded panels printed in PETG, a groove at every fold; the lamp has windows), `fold_lantern` (that lamp at 420 mm in plywood, every face a part of its own, laced), and for the drawn spacing curve `vase` (stacked), `ripple` (interlocked), `berry` (radial), `hook` (curve) | generated by `uv run python -m core.testmodels examples/` | none; use them for anything |
| `cow_spot` | *Spot* by Keenan Crane, [3D Model Repository](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/ModelRepository/) | CC0 1.0, public domain |

`presets.json` says what each one opens with in the app: the technique that suits the shape, a `rotate` that stands
y-up scans upright, and parameters that slice without an error (`tests/test_presets.py` checks every one).
